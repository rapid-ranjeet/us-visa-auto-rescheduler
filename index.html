How FalconPath works

It watches the US visa scheduling portal for dates inside your range and can book the earliest match auto-vipmatically. No credits, no subscription.

Any CGI Federal scheduling site on usvisascheduling.com — India, Australia, UAE, Türkiye.

1. Sign in to the portal.
2. Open the OFC and Consular pages once so locations load.
3. Pick locations, set a date range and interval.
4. Press Start scanning and leave the tab open.

With auto-vip-book on it books the earliest matching time. Turn it off to be notified and confirm yourself.
