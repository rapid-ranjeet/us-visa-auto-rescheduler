Help & Frequently Asked Questions

FalconPath Visa Assistant monitors the US visa appointment system around the clock, scanning for earlier available dates. When a slot within your preferred date range appears, it can automatically book it for you — saving hours of manual checking.

FalconPath supports appointment rescheduling for the CGI-based US visa scheduling system used in:
  • India
  • Australia
  • United Arab Emirates
  • Türkiye
Open Appointment Portal →

Once activated, the scheduler periodically checks for open appointment slots at your chosen consulate. The "Scan Interval" setting controls how often it checks. Each successful date check uses one credit from your balance.

1. Sign in to your visa appointment portal
2. Open this extension from the side panel
3. Toggle "Scheduler Active" to start scanning
4. Set your preferred date range
5. The extension will notify you when a slot is found

Use "Clear All Settings" to reset everything if needed.
