How FalconPath works

It watches the US visa scheduling portal for open dates inside the range you pick, and can auto-book the earliest match. No credits, no subscriptions — just a browser extension.

Any CGI Federal scheduling site built on usvisascheduling.com: India, Australia, United Arab Emirates, Türkiye. Open the portal →

1. Sign in to the portal (the assistant fills it once you give it your details).
2. Visit the OFC and Consular scheduling pages once so the city lists populate.
3. Pick cities, set your date range, choose a scan interval.
4. Hit Start scanning and leave the tab open.

With Auto-book on, the extension picks the earliest matching time and confirms it. With it off, it just notifies you and lets you confirm manually.
